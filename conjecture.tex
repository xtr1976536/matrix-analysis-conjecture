\section{Problem}
Let $U\in\mathbb R^{n\times k}$ have orthonormal columns. The Goreinov--Tyrtyshnikov--Zamarashkin hypothesis asks whether there is a $k\times k$ row submatrix $Q$ with $\sigma_{\min}(Q)\ge n^{-1/2}$. This repository records exploratory work on the case $k=3$ and related bounds; the unrestricted case remains open in these notes.
\section{Status}
The arguments and examples are provisional and are being discussed with researchers in matrix analysis. They should not be read as a finished theorem.
